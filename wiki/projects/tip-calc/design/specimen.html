1. Ledger slip recommended

Feels like the bottom of the check: dot-leader rows, numerals in a mono face right-aligned to one edge, warm paper, the answer in ballpoint blue. Plex Mono for every number, Plex Sans for labels. One ink, one blue.

Wrong for: a product that should feel playful, or a dark-mode-first one.

2. Lit instrument

A backlit readout in a dim room: near-black ground, one oversized amber figure at the top, inputs as quiet dark fields underneath. A condensed mono display face, amber used only on the readout.

Wrong for: daylight on a patio, and anything that should show its arithmetic. The readout hides the working, so the rounding rule goes unseen.

3. Chunky blocks

A playful instrument: yellow ground, thick ink borders, hard offset shadows, blocks that press down with a springy motion. A heavy grotesque for display.

Wrong for: a finished utility that should disappear after ten seconds. Springy motion fights "instant".

Sketches show layout and palette only; type is held to Plex Mono for all three. Why 1: its layout is the arithmetic (bill, tip, total, then each), so the brief's rounding rule is visible, and a blank field is a blank line, never "0.00". What would change this: the owner finds the paper glaring in a dark restaurant (then add a dark scheme), or the human-evidence gate shows nobody reads the ledger rows (then direction 2).

Label 12px caps · data 20px · body 16px

Plex Mono is deliberate: a printed-check face, fixed-width digits so a changing figure never shakes, and the dotted zero separates 0 from O in a field a thumb is about to edit. Plex Sans carries only the quiet words. Fonts are self-hosted woff2 (about 53 KB for three files) because the page must work offline; no CDN.

Color

Ink on paper. Blue is the pen: it marks only the answer, the preset you chose and the focus ring. Red appears only when input is invalid.

Press a preset: 100ms press, 150ms colour change

One easing: ease-out, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). No springs, no bounce. Micro for press, short for colour and focus, medium for an error appearing, long unused. The result figure never animates: it changes at once, because reading it is the job. Reduced motion sets every duration to 0 and nothing depends on movement to be understood.

Voice

Plain · exact · quiet. Say what to do or what happened, in the fewest words, and never apologise or cheer.
